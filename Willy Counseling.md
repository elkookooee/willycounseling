About Cecilia

## A Therapist Who Meets You Where You Are

Cecilia Williams is a Licensed Professional Counselor and the heart behind Willy Counseling, PLLC. A Mexican-American from Dallas, TX, she brings both clinical expertise and genuine warmth to every session.

With experience across inpatient adolescent and adult settings, addiction recovery centers, and outpatient individual, couples, and family therapy, Cecilia's approach is collaborative and deeply human.

Her primary framework is Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), supplemented by attachment-focused interventions tailored to each client's unique needs.

**BS** Psychology & Child Learning & Development — UT Dallas

**MA** Counseling — University of North Texas

The Foundation

## A Therapy Built on Three Core Beliefs

Every session is grounded in principles that foster lasting growth and emotional wellbeing.

Self-Compassion

### Self-Compassion

When we learn to be our own best friend, we become better equipped to cope through the hard times of life. Self-compassion is the foundation of every breakthrough.

### Therapeutic Relationship

The bond between you and your therapist should model a healthy, safe, and secure relationship — one that provides a template for all your relationships.

Meeting Emotional Needs

### Meeting Emotional Needs

Before we can ask others to meet our emotional needs, we must first learn how to meet them ourselves. This self-awareness transforms how we relate to everyone around us.
